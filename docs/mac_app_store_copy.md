# Mac App Store copy

## Promotional text

Turn photos into polished, subject-aware collages on Mac. Explore flexible layouts, refine every crop, save editable projects, and export at up to 8K.

## Description

Turn photos into one story with MixaFrame, a subject-aware collage studio designed for Mac.

MixaFrame finds important subjects, recommends layouts that fit your photos, and gives you direct control over every crop. Import images from Finder, build on a spacious live canvas, and save each collage as an editable project you can revisit anytime.

LAYOUTS THAT FIT

Start with Smart Layout or explore Grid, Featured, Mosaic, Slanted, and Flow families. Choose compositions that emphasize one, two, or three lead photos, then adjust spacing, canvas corners, and dividers until the story feels balanced.

SUBJECTS STAY IN VIEW

On-device analysis finds faces and prominent subjects so automatic crops begin in a useful place. Fine-tune horizontal and vertical focus, zoom, reorder photos, or return to the suggested arrangement.

EDIT WITH THE COMPLETE COLLAGE IN VIEW

Use the Mac workspace to compare layouts and refine individual photos alongside the full composition. Switch canvas shapes, choose a light or dark background, and work with up to 12 photos.

PROJECTS, NOT THROWAWAY EXPORTS

Organize related work in collections. Each saved project keeps its photos, layout, order, crops, canvas style, resolution, format, and quality settings ready for your next edit.

EXPORT YOUR WAY

Save through the standard macOS save panel as JPEG, PNG, Apple HEIF, or WebP. Choose Space Saver, Balanced, or Best Quality and export with a longest edge up to 8192 pixels.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Subject analysis happens on your Mac. MixaFrame stores selected photo copies and projects locally and does not upload your photos to a MixaFrame server.

CREATE FOR FREE

Every collage creation and editing feature is available without a subscription. Free exports include a visible MixaFrame watermark. MixaFrame Premium is an optional auto-renewable annual subscription that removes the watermark from exported images. Eligible new subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. You can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel your subscription through Apple.
